LifeOS Privacy Policy

LifeOS is a private application used only by its owner.

  • With the owner's permission, LifeOS reads the owner's own sleep and health data from Google to show it in the owner's personal diary.
  • The data is stored only on the owner's own server and in the owner's own Google Sheets.
  • The data is not sold, shared with or transferred to anyone else, and is not used for advertising.
  • Access can be revoked at any time in the Google Account settings (Security → Third-party access).
